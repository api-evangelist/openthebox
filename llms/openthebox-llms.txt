# openthebox

openthebox provides Belgian company intelligence for banking, private equity, compliance, and business development teams.

## What openthebox is for

- Search and analyze Belgian companies and people
- Map ownership structures with Spiderweb
- Track changes with alerts and notifications
- Support KYC, AML, due diligence, deal sourcing, benchmarking, and portfolio monitoring
- Access company data through the platform and API
